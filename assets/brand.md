# Classic brand

Classic is an independent, Rust-built design editor. Its mark pairs an open canvas frame with a folded artboard and a small light point: the frame suggests an adaptable workspace, while the point marks the moment a design comes into focus.

The symbol uses a dark ink field with a violet-to-teal frame, a pale artboard, and a warm yellow accent. It is original artwork and does not use Figma's logo or wordmark. Keep the mark clear of other graphics; the SVG has a transparent background outside its rounded field and scales cleanly at small and large sizes.
