Privacy notice

How the static website and local desktop application handle information.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This is a project notice for the current local-first beta. It must be reviewed and updated by the project operator before a hosted service is launched.

Local desktop application

Classic Studio stores project data in local .classic files and may save local recovery snapshots. The desktop editor does not require a hosted account for its current local workflows.

Website

This static site is designed to work without analytics, advertising pixels, or marketing cookies. Standard server logs may be produced by whichever hosting provider operates the site; their retention and processing are controlled by that provider and the site operator.

Future hosted services

Optional collaboration, authentication, AI assistance, and release delivery services need their own published data inventory, retention schedule, access controls, and contact address before they are enabled. Do not upload confidential data to an unreleased service.

Your choices

You can keep work local, delete local project and recovery files, and decline optional services. For a live service, the operator must publish a contact route for privacy requests and applicable regional rights.
